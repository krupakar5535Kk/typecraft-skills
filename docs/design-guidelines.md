# TypeCraft Skills — Design Guidelines

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines define the quality principles for applying typography styles and design templates through TypeCraft.

## 2. Typography Principles

- Establish a clear typographic hierarchy.
- Prioritize readability and legibility.
- Use typefaces appropriate to the selected visual direction.
- Maintain deliberate spacing, alignment, and proportion.
- Avoid unnecessary decorative effects.
- Preserve the intended personality of the selected style.

## 3. Composition Principles

- Establish a clear focal point.
- Organize information according to importance.
- Use consistent alignment and spacing.
- Maintain visual balance.
- Use negative space intentionally.
- Avoid overcrowding the composition.

## 4. Style and Template Independence

Typography styles define visual personality and treatment.

Design templates define structural arrangement and content composition.

They may be used independently or combined. Combining them must not erase the identity of either resource.

## 5. Domain Adaptation

### Static Graphics
Prioritize hierarchy, composition, contrast, and legibility at the final display size.

### Motion Graphics
Consider timing, movement, transitions, and readability throughout animation.

### Video
Maintain safe placement, readable text duration, and consistency across sequences.

### Web and UI
Prioritize responsive behavior, accessibility, interface hierarchy, and screen readability.

### Editorial
Maintain typographic consistency, grid discipline, reading flow, and publication hierarchy.

## 6. Accessibility

- Maintain sufficient text and background contrast.
- Avoid relying on color alone to communicate meaning.
- Preserve readable text sizes.
- Consider users with visual and reading difficulties.
- Avoid motion effects that interfere with comprehension.

## 7. Production Quality

Before finalizing a design, check:

1. Typography hierarchy.
2. Readability and legibility.
3. Alignment and spacing.
4. Color and contrast.
5. Composition and balance.
6. Domain-specific requirements.
7. Technical output constraints.

## 8. Creative Integrity

Respect explicit user instructions and selected resources.

Do not silently replace a requested style or template.

When a requested effect cannot be achieved with available tools, explain the limitation and provide the closest practical alternative.
