# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone contributing to TypeCraft Skills.

We welcome contributors regardless of experience, background, identity, or skill level.

## Expected Behavior

All community members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Communicate constructively.
- Welcome different perspectives and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project.
- Respect other contributors and their work.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting or degrading language.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the contact method published in the repository.

Reports will be reviewed and handled appropriately.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

They may also restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, contributions, discussions, and community interactions associated with TypeCraft Skills.
